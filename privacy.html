Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains the information that may be submitted through ChefNearby and how it may be used to respond to service enquiries.

Information collected

Booking forms may collect your name, phone number, email, city, locality, requested service, date and time, guest or family size, cuisine preferences, budget and notes you choose to provide.

How information is used

We use submitted information to review enquiries, contact you about service availability, coordinate possible provider options, troubleshoot the website and protect the service against misuse.

Service providers and infrastructure

Information may be processed by technical service providers that host the website, store booking requests or support communications. We should share only the information reasonably needed to respond to your enquiry.

Retention and security

Information may be retained as needed to respond to requests, maintain business records, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. No website or online transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

Avoid submitting passwords, payment PINs, government ID numbers or unnecessary sensitive details in the enquiry form. For a request to correct or remove information, contact ChefNearby through the contact details shown on the website.

Cookies and analytics

Analytics and advertising tags may be added in the future. When enabled, the relevant settings and disclosures should be updated to reflect the actual tools and applicable consent requirements.

Children

Do not submit personal information about a child unless it is necessary for a service enquiry and you are authorised to provide it.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the service changes. Before full commercial launch, add the responsible legal entity name, a monitored privacy contact and any required jurisdiction-specific disclosures.
