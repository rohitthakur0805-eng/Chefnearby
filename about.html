What we do

We provide a digital place to describe a service requirement, preferred date, location, guest count and other details. Requests are reviewed for availability and suitable partner options. A submitted request is not itself a confirmed booking.

Where we operate

Bengaluru is our primary launch market. Customers in other listed Indian cities or international locations can send an enquiry, but availability, local partners, pricing, taxes, payment methods and launch status must be confirmed individually. A city appearing on this website does not guarantee active service coverage there.

Our approach

We aim to make service discovery clear, keep booking requests organised and share practical menu and service information. Provider identity, qualifications, availability and final service terms should be confirmed before any engagement.
