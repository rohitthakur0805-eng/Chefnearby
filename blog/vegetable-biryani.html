Vegetable Biryani Recipe

A guide to fragrant vegetable biryani, layering, whole spices and vegetarian menu planning.

Ingredients overview

Vegetable biryani commonly uses basmati rice, mixed vegetables, onions, ginger, garlic, whole spices, herbs, yoghurt or a suitable alternative, and saffron or other aromatics if desired.

Portions: scale ingredient quantities to your guest count; this guide is not a gram-by-gram production sheet.

Preparation method

Par-cook rice separately. Prepare a seasoned vegetable base, layer rice and vegetables with herbs and aromatics, then finish covered on low heat until the rice is tender. Rest before fluffing and serving.

Dietary and allergy notes

Check dairy in yoghurt or ghee, gluten in packaged spice blends, and any nut garnish. Verify whether the kitchen can prevent cross-contact for severe allergies.

This guide is general information, not medical advice. Customers should disclose allergies and confirm ingredients and cross-contact controls with the person preparing the food.
