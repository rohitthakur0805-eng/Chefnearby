Paneer Butter Masala Recipe

A practical paneer butter masala recipe guide with ingredient checklist, cooking method and dietary notes.

Ingredients overview

Paneer butter masala is a popular North Indian curry made with paneer in a tomato-based sauce. Prepare onion, tomato, ginger, garlic, spices, paneer and your chosen dairy or dairy-free alternative. Scale quantities to the number of servings.

Portions: scale ingredient quantities to your guest count; this guide is not a gram-by-gram production sheet.

Preparation method

Sauté aromatics and spices, cook the tomato base until concentrated, blend if desired, return to the pan, simmer with paneer and finish with cream or a suitable alternative. Adjust seasoning before serving.

Dietary and allergy notes

Common checks include dairy in paneer and cream, tree nuts if cashews are used, and gluten cross-contact in spice mixes. Confirm every ingredient and allergy control with the cook.

This guide is general information, not medical advice. Customers should disclose allergies and confirm ingredients and cross-contact controls with the person preparing the food.
