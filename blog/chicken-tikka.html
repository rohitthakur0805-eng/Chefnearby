Chicken Tikka Recipe Guide

A chicken tikka preparation guide covering marinade, cooking options and allergy checks.

Ingredients overview

Chicken tikka commonly uses boneless chicken, yoghurt, ginger, garlic, lemon, chilli and ground spices. Scale the marinade to the portion size and heat preference.

Portions: scale ingredient quantities to your guest count; this guide is not a gram-by-gram production sheet.

Preparation method

Marinate chilled chicken, arrange on skewers or a tray, then cook using a suitable high-heat method until the thickest pieces reach a safe internal temperature. Rest briefly before serving.

Dietary and allergy notes

Yoghurt contains dairy; spice blends may contain mustard or traces of other allergens. Keep raw poultry separate from ready-to-eat food and verify safe cooking.

This guide is general information, not medical advice. Customers should disclose allergies and confirm ingredients and cross-contact controls with the person preparing the food.
