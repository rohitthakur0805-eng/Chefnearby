Italian Tomato Basil Pasta Recipe

A simple tomato and basil pasta guide with menu ideas and dietary considerations.

Ingredients overview

A classic tomato basil pasta can use pasta, ripe tomatoes or passata, olive oil, garlic, basil, salt and pepper. Parmesan is optional and can be omitted or replaced to suit dietary preferences.

Portions: scale ingredient quantities to your guest count; this guide is not a gram-by-gram production sheet.

Preparation method

Cook pasta according to its package instructions. Warm garlic gently in olive oil, add tomato and simmer until balanced, season, then toss with pasta and fresh basil. Finish with cheese only if suitable.

Dietary and allergy notes

Pasta usually contains wheat/gluten. Cheese contains dairy. Packaged pasta, sauces and toppings may have other allergens; confirm labels and cross-contact controls.

This guide is general information, not medical advice. Customers should disclose allergies and confirm ingredients and cross-contact controls with the person preparing the food.
